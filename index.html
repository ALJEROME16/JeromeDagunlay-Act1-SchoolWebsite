The 4 most Important People in MFNHS

Mr. Moises H. Aquino

– The pioneering first principal who provided the initial leadership during the school's early years, keeping it alive during financial hardships and setting the stage for its eventual nationalization in 1983.

Dr. Vergilia O. Pancho

current Secondary School Principal II (appointed in 2025), who leads the overall administration, manages the faculty, and oversees the campus today.

Sir Teodoro "Ted" P. Casiano

former Secondary School Principal II who led MFNHS for multiple years through its growth as a "mega school" and spearheaded major institutional partnerships.

Dr. Veronica P. Polig

– A distinguished former school head/principal who led MFNHS during critical transition periods, earning the school recognition as an outstanding institution during regional assessments.
